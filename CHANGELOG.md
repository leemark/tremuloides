# Changelog

## v0.3.0 (2026-10-01)
- New Quake lens: time-displacement that smears trembling aspen leaves into flowing gold
- Modes: Rows, Columns, Radial, Luma (bright parts lag) and Slit-scan
- Shutter records a short burst; in Slit-scan, tap to start, pan, and tap to stop

## v0.2.0 (2026-10-01)
- New Ink & Wash lens, now the default: painterly brushwork with inked edges
- Four palettes: Auto (from the scene), San Juan, Gouache and Mono ink
- Flow line style for longer, brushier ink strokes, plus optional paper texture
- Quality setting (Fast / Balanced / Best) for a smoother live preview

## v0.1.0 (2026-10-01)
- First release: offline camera with Original and Posterize lenses
- Gallery with share, save, re-edit and delete
- Import photos from your phone and apply lenses
- Works fully offline once installed, with a reload banner for updates
- Diagnostics you can copy into a bug report
